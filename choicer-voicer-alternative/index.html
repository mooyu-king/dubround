Bring a compatible Pack — not assumptions

Choicer Voicer community Packs vary: some contain character reactions, some contain music or interface sounds, and others include timed dialogue and video. DubRound's directory is a collection of source listings, not a promise that every file is playable or every line has a transcript.

  1. Inspect before loading. Open the Pack page and check its author, archive size, available previews and compatibility notes. Retain the original source link.
  2. Listen to the prepared reference. Confirm that it is a useful voice clip rather than a score sound or backing track. If decoding fails, try the built-in Demo instead.
  3. Record one short take. Use headphones, check microphone permission and compare repeated attempts against the same reference. A successful import alone does not prove recording or scoring works.

For a ZIP you already have, the Pack ZIP editor offers local editing of supported content. It is ZIP-only; do not rename a RAR or 7z file to ZIP. Check archive-specific loading on its community page.

Try a WiiVoice sound collection, inspect the Think Mark scene cues, or make a Pack from your own video. Only use and share material you have permission to use.

How to start your first DubRound challenge

  1. Open the game menu. Choose Play DubRound. The link does not start recording or skip the menu.
  2. Choose your material. Select Play Demo, or Play Pack for supported material. Wait for preparation and inspect the available reference before continuing.
  3. Prepare your microphone. Follow the player setup, use headphones and allow browser microphone access when you choose to record. If the input is silent, use the microphone test.
  4. Listen, record and review. Play the reference, record your take, then inspect its feedback. Retry or keep the take before moving on. Silence or unsuitable input may not receive a usable score.

These steps describe the existing controls. They are not a claim that this session performed a new device or microphone test.

Want to replace a video's voice instead?

A dub game challenges your performance against a reference. A video editor changes the soundtrack. Use DubRound's Hotel Lobby-style video dubbing walkthrough and studio when you want to record new segments, correct their captions, preview the replacement and export a before-and-after video. It does not generate AI characters or guarantee automatic lip sync.

Before you switch games

Is DubRound the official Choicer Voicer online game?

No. DubRound is an independent browser game, not an official port, sequel or download mirror. The original The Choicer Voicer is made by YeahMaybe; use the author's itch.io page for the original game.

Can I play a dub game without installing a desktop game?

Yes. Open DubRound and choose Play Demo to try its built-in Pack. You need a microphone and browser permission to record. Community downloads, audio decoding and browser capabilities can affect whether another Pack works.

Will every Choicer Voicer Pack work?

No. An archive format is only the container. DubRound must recognize the file layout and decode the audio. Menus, judges and original game modes are not recreated by importing their files. Check a Pack's own preview and compatibility notes.

Can friends hear my microphone in an online room?

The room synchronizes turns, readiness and scores, not a live microphone call. Each player needs the same Pack on their device. Online play also requires an available room service; local parties use one shared device.

Is DubRound scoring more accurate than The Choicer Voicer?

We have not run a controlled accuracy comparison. DubRound compares audio features such as timing, energy, rhythm and pitch. Its feedback is for practice and friendly competition; it does not verify words or identify the original speaker.

Can I save a dubbed video instead of a Pack?

The game exports a Pack ZIP of accepted recordings and related results. The separate Video Studio replaces selected audio segments and can export a before-and-after MP4 on supported browsers. These are different workflows; video export is not part of importing a Pack.

How do I use my own Pack?

Open Play Pack in DubRound, or use the local Pack ZIP editor to inspect and edit supported ZIP content. Keep your source archive. A RAR or 7z file cannot be converted by renaming it, and an edited DubRound ZIP is not guaranteed to work in the original game.
