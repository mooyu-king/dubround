BEFORE YOU RECORD

Voice game questions

How do I play with friends?

Choose a local party on one device or a 2–4 player online room when the room service is available. Everyone in an online room loads the same Pack, gets ready and takes a turn. Compare scores in the room; this is friendly competition, not a live microphone call.

Can I turn my own video into a Pack?

Yes. Open the video-to-Pack maker, choose media you have permission to use, prepare the spoken clips and captions, then export a Pack ZIP. Import it into DubRound or give the same Pack to your friends for a room challenge.

What is DubRound?

DubRound is a free browser voice game. Listen to a reference, record your take, and compare the timing and sound pattern. A round is one short performance challenge.

Do I need an account or an app to play?

You can start the basic game in your browser without an account or a desktop download. Recording requires a microphone and browser permission.

Can I use Choicer Voicer packs?

DubRound can prepare supported community archives for its own game. Check each Pack's format and preview before playing. Voice collections, menu sounds and timed dubbing scenes are different kinds of content; importing audio does not reproduce every feature of the original desktop game.

What can I export?

Finish a Demo or compatible third-party Pack and choose Export my Pack. Your ZIP includes accepted recordings for replay, score JSON files, five-axis radar SVG images, and the saved attempts from that session, including retakes. It is an audio Pack, not an MP4 video.

Are retakes saved automatically?

Recordings and their scoring results are saved locally in this browser, including retakes. Browser storage is device-specific and can be cleared; export your ZIP to keep a separate copy.

Can I play my own recordings again?

Yes. The takes you confirm and keep become the playable lines in your exported Pack. Import that ZIP back into DubRound to use your own performance as the next reference.

Does my recording leave the browser?

Basic recording and scoring run locally. Export downloads a file to your device. Any separate sharing feature should explain its upload before you choose it.

What if the microphone does not work?

Check the microphone test, allow access in your browser's site settings, and check your system input device. Desktop Chrome or Edge is the recommended starting point; device support should be checked before a long session.
