What an online room shares

The current room workflow shares names, Pack identity, readiness, turns and casual scores. It does not stream your microphone to other players. A shared scoreboard is not a live voice call. Recordings and Pack files stay on each player's device unless they use a separate, explicit sharing action.

Before the host starts

  1. Choose a short compatible Pack and agree whether retries are allowed.
  2. Each player checks their microphone, then stops the test.
  3. Online players load the exact same audio and line order. Different versions cannot mark ready.
  4. The host starts when all 2–4 players are connected and ready.

If someone disconnects

Keep the tab open and reconnect. A disconnected player has a grace period; each turn also has a time limit. The room panel reports whose turn it is. Scores are calculated locally and are for friendly play, not cheat-proof competition. If the service cannot reconnect, return to a local round.
