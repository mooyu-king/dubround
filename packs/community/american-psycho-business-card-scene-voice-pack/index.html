How to practice with this Pack

Choose a clearly spoken reference and make two takes: first follow the timing, then refine the emphasis without changing the pace. Leave the pauses intact. In a local group, compare the same cue across players before switching to another speaker; the five source roles do not require five players.

Compatibility and preparation

The ZIP is about 328 MiB and may take longer to prepare on memory-limited devices. Its icon.png contains JPEG data; DubRound now recognizes that supported image content while retaining an import warning. Two reference cues did not meet the current scoring-analysis threshold, so choose another spoken cue if a score cannot be produced.

Why can a cue play but not produce a score?

Decoding audio and finding enough usable voice for scoring are different checks. Two references in this inspected release did not meet the analysis threshold; use a clearer spoken cue rather than repeatedly recording over an unsuitable reference.

Does the image warning mean the whole Pack is broken?

Not in the inspected release. A source icon is labelled PNG but contains JPEG data. The importer accepts the recognized raster image and records a warning; all 19 voice cues decoded in the local Chrome check.

What if loading fails?

Retry the source download or import your copy locally. Formats and browser decoders differ. You can return to the built-in game without waiting for this archive.

What can I save after recording?

DubRound can export accepted recordings and line data in a Pack ZIP. This does not create an MP4 movie. Keep your source and only publish material you have permission to share.
