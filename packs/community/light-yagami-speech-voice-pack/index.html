How to practice with this Pack

Start with a short response before taking on a longer passage. Divide the longer speech into natural breath groups and retain its pauses instead of rushing through the words. On a retry, change one aspect of the delivery, such as the ending emphasis, so the comparison is useful.

Compatibility and preparation

The ZIP is about 36 MiB. Fourteen cues decoded in the inspected release, but one reference did not meet the scoring-analysis threshold. If that cue cannot be scored, choose a longer clearly voiced reference; louder recording alone cannot repair an unsuitable reference.

Does this Pack contain only Light's voice?

No. The inspected source labels both Light and Ryuk. Preview the selected cue rather than assuming every entry belongs to the character in the title.

Do I have to record the entire speech at once?

No. Start with an individual imported cue. Practicing a manageable passage makes it easier to compare breathing, pauses and repeated takes.

What if loading fails?

Retry the source download or import your copy locally. Formats and browser decoders differ. You can return to the built-in game without waiting for this archive.

What can I save after recording?

DubRound can export accepted recordings and line data in a Pack ZIP. This does not create an MP4 movie. Keep your source and only publish material you have permission to share.
