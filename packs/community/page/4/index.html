HOW TO PLAY / COMMUNITY PACKS

From card to round.

Choicer Voicer Packs: compatibility with DubRound

Looking for Choicer Voicer Packs? These are credited community source listings, not official DubRound releases. Source views are historical popularity signals, not DubRound plays or compatibility ratings. A listed archive, mapped captions and a completed recording test are different levels of evidence.

Use each detail page's preview and dated check notes. See what carries over to DubRound, or edit a supported local ZIP. Renaming a RAR or 7z file does not convert it to ZIP.

Use the source format and previews to choose material. Packs with a direct archive can load in the browser; other listings explain how to get and import your own copy.

  1. 01Open a PackSelect Original source to visit the original listing, or Preview & play to check the Pack on DubRound.
  2. 02Download automaticallyCheck the preview and file details, then start loading from the Pack page. Download progress stays visible.
  3. 03Load into the gameWait for supported audio to be prepared, then inspect the clips.
  4. 04Play and recordStart a round and record your take. Accepted recordings can be exported as a local Pack ZIP.
