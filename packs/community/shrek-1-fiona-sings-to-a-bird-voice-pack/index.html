How to practice with this Pack

Listen for the duration and shape of one vowel sound before trying it. Stay within a comfortable vocal range and microphone level; you do not need to force a high note or maximum volume. For a local group, repeat the same reference so everyone is comparing the same phrase length.

Compatibility and preparation

This ZIP is about 35 MiB. DubRound can analyze the imported references, but it is not a dedicated karaoke judge or a guarantee of precise singing assessment. The source speaker label does not establish a separate playable role for every sound heard in the scene.
