How to practice with this Pack

Choose an opening statement and keep its measured pace, then practice a response with a different emphasis. Do not remove pauses just to finish faster. For a local group, try one cue per player before changing roles so the recording feedback compares similar material.

Compatibility and preparation

The ZIP is about 43 MiB. All 19 imported references decoded locally, but one did not meet the scoring-analysis threshold. A playable clip is not automatically suitable for scoring; use another clear spoken reference if the game cannot analyze it.
