How to practice with this Pack

Choose one short exchange and follow the rhythm of the setup before trying the response. Keep the comic timing clear without exaggerating every word. In a local party, use the same cue for each player first, then switch source roles once everyone is familiar with the reference.

Compatibility and preparation

The ZIP is about 16 MiB. Its six source speaker labels are reference roles, not a six-player requirement or a statement about the complete episode cast. DubRound compares the selected audio performance; importing the Pack does not recreate the original game's menus.

How is this different from Bubble Argument?

This release imports 22 cues with Plankton and other source-labelled roles. Bubble Argument imports 31 cues labelled SpongeBob, Patrick and Squidward. They are separate source Packs with different selections.

Are six people needed to play the six roles?

No. Practice solo or let local players take turns. The source role list describes the references, not the number of participants required.

What if loading fails?

Retry the source download or import your copy locally. Formats and browser decoders differ. You can return to the built-in game without waiting for this archive.

What can I save after recording?

DubRound can export accepted recordings and line data in a Pack ZIP. This does not create an MP4 movie. Keep your source and only publish material you have permission to share.
