HOW TO USE A PACK

From files to the next round.

A round is a short challenge: hear the reference, record your take and compare the result.

  1. 01Prepare the PackDownload a community ZIP or create clips from your own media.
  2. 02Import or renderValidate the downloaded files, or render your selected custom lines.
  3. 03Preview the RoundListen to each selected prompt and confirm the order.
  4. 04Play or exportPlay the prepared clips, or save a Pack ZIP. Check the chosen export format before using it in another app.
THIS BROWSERInstalled Packs stay on this device and browser unless you download the ZIP.

Turn compatible clips into a playable Round.

  4. 04PlayPerform the Round or export a compatible Pack.
