PRIVACY POLICY

What happens behind the glass.

This policy explains the limited processing involved when you use DubRound. Last updated: September 16, 2026.

Local media and microphone

Creation tools, Mic Test and the starter Round process selected media and microphone audio in your browser by default. Temporary recordings are not automatically uploaded. Browser permission is controlled by you and can be revoked in site settings.

Optional online rooms

When you create or join an online room, the room service receives your chosen name, Pack title, an audio-content fingerprint, line count, readiness and scores. Room members can see names, connection status and results. ZIP files, recordings and transcripts are not automatically transferred. A room-specific reconnect credential stays in this tab’s session storage; do not share it. Development rooms are held in server memory and expire after ten minutes without activity or when the service restarts. Scores are calculated on players’ devices and are intended for casual play, not verified competition.

Audience measurement

We load https://data.1back.link/js/app.js with data-domain=dubround.com and endpoint https://data.1back.link/api/send. We record aggregate visits and limited interaction events such as navigation, game start, tool activation, permission outcome and coarse file-size ranges. We do not include microphone audio, filenames, captions, player names or free-form text in analytics events.

Advertising and external services

Advertising scripts may process technical data, cookies or similar identifiers under their own policies. Embedded games and YouTube videos are delivered by third parties, which receive normal request information when you choose to load them. DubRound cannot inspect a cross-origin game's microphone stream or internal storage.

Community results

Some game pages may offer an optional leaderboard. A submitted display name, score, time and proof screenshot may become public. Do not upload faces, notifications, conversations, account details or room codes. Submission requires explicit consent and removal can be requested by emailing mooyuking@gmail.com.

Local storage and migration files

Packs, editor state, party settings and result history may be stored in this browser. Export and import tools create or read a local JSON file; they do not upload it. Clear site data to remove locally stored entries.

Voice effects and comparison tools

The voice changer, optional game take effects and score comparison decode selected audio locally. They do not upload your audio or provide a live microphone to another application. Files remain in page memory until cleared, replaced or the page is closed; downloaded WAV files remain on your device. Effect-selection analytics use preset identifiers, not audio or filenames.

Creation drafts and layout settings

When you choose Save draft locally, the creator stores your source media, clip edits and optional cover in this browser's local database. Restore and delete controls affect that saved draft; deleting it does not delete downloaded files or your current editor. A social-layout settings download is a local JSON file, not a published video. Clearing browser site data can remove drafts and Packs.

Your choices

You may decline microphone access, block third-party scripts, avoid optional uploads, clear browser data or leave the page. For privacy questions contact mooyuking@gmail.com.
