Before you record

Is this a live voice changer?

No. You record first and apply the effect afterward. It does not change the microphone in another game, a call or Discord.

Does this clone a person's voice?

No. These are simple audio effects, not identity cloning or character voice models.

Where is my audio stored?

The tool processes it in this page's memory. It is not uploaded or saved as a cloud draft. Download a WAV before leaving if you want to keep it.

Why did the pitch effect change the length?

Deep and Bright use slower or faster playback. Robot and Echo use different processing. Always compare and preview before saving.
